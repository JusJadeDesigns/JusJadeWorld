About the studio

Jus Jade Designs is a one-woman studio out of St. Kitts & Nevis, building event graphics for the Eastern Caribbean's promoters, DJs, and food brands.

Over 7+ years, the work has covered fete flyers, boat ride promos, artist tour graphics, Carnival and Sugar Mas campaigns, and brand identities for restaurants and lounges — always built around what's actually happening on the ground: the music, the food, the culture.
